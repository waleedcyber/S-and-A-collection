🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we handle your information.

Last updated: June 2025

📋 Overview

S&S Collection ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it when you shop with us at s-and-s-collection.onrender.com.

📦 Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order details and payment reference (not your card details)

We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, or any sensitive payment credentials. All payments are processed securely by Paystack.

🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Contact you about your order status
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our products and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

🤝 Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only with:

  • Paystack — to process your payments securely
  • Delivery partners — to fulfill your order when applicable
  • Legal authorities — if required by law

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to save your shopping cart between visits. No tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies are used on this site.

🔐 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Our site uses HTTPS encryption, and passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

🧒 Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

✏️ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for us to use your data at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
